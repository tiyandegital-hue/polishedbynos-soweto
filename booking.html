Atelier Decorum

House Rules & Atelier Decorum

The studio's own rules, kept plain. Please read them before you book.

01

Operating Days & Closed Days

Open Tuesday to Saturday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.

02

Strict Punctuality

Late coming is strictly not tolerated. Arrive 5 minutes prior to your chair time.

03

Salon Sanctuary

No kids, extra guests, partners, or alcohol in the salon. Solo client appointments only.

04

Clean & Healthy Nails

Arrive with clean, natural bare nails. Soak-off removal of any previous work is required before your appointment.
